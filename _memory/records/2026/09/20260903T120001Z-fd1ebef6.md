## Decision

Evaluators may return **PASS (with notes)** when every finding is classified `minor`. **NEEDS FIXES** is reserved for at least one `major` or `blocker`. Minors are listed under `### Notes (non-blocking)` and are never dropped silently.

## Why

Cycle-1 **NEEDS FIXES** on wording-level issues doubled spawns per task and billed frontier models for non-behavioral nits. The token-efficiency review showed evaluation cost was a first-class spend line.

## Rejected alternatives

- Keeping a single PASS/NEEDS FIXES split with implicit tolerance for minors (no distinct verdict).
- Dropping minor findings entirely on PASS (loses audit trail).

## Consequences

Task-criteria and all six canonical agent templates define `minor` consistently with evaluator and ORCHESTRATION.md (escalation-ladder check 2 keeps the failure-class taxonomy line character-identical). Orchestrators must route fix cycles only when majors/blockers appear; notes do not trigger implementer respawn by themselves.
