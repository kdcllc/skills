Pitfall: an acceptance criterion that scans emit goldens for an old phrase cannot pass if it includes the error-* cases.

Those cases are error-manifest-corrupt, error-manifest-no-answers and error-upgrade-blocked under tests/agent-kit/fixtures/emit-golden/cases/. They restore their .prior tree and then the emit fails (run_case_emit in tests/agent-kit/templates/emit-golden.sh), so their .golden files equal the seeded prior install, and `emit-golden.sh --update` never rewrites them. Hand-editing those goldens fails the suite, and editing .prior files changes the fixture's meaning.

How it was detected: the implementer reported the 12 files as unchangeable. The criterion was corrected by amendment to exclude the error-* cases, with a separate check that their goldens stay unchanged.

How to avoid: write golden-scanning criteria with `':(exclude,glob)tests/agent-kit/fixtures/emit-golden/cases/error-*/**'`. Classify error-case goldens as unaffected in the consumer trace. Make true-negative samples include a golden from every case family.
