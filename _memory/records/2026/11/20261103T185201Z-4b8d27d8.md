Pitfall: asking a test-writer for "one regression proof per requirement" leaves sub-assertions unproven when one requirement adds several checks.

What happened (goal regression-proof-resume, task 06b): requirement T2 added three assertions to one suite case: at least 8 step-3 sub-bullets, `HEAD at this write` present, and `phase:` on at least 3 lines. The orchestrator's context package asked for "one record per pin T1, T2, T3, T4". The writer proved T2 with a sub-bullet mutation, and the orchestrator's independent re-runs proved the sub-bullet and HEAD checks. Nobody proved the `phase:` count check, which sat exactly at its boundary (3 matches). The task evaluator returned NEEDS FIXES citing F3.

How detected: the evaluator mapped each new assertion in the suite diff to a proof record.

Avoid it: list the new assertions from the suite diff and require one proof per assertion. For count thresholds, mutate exactly at the boundary (3 to 2) with a same-line edit outside the windows the other assertions read, and record the precondition count. The fix was evidence only: a terminal scratch-kit run, recorded in criteria-evidence.md, then a resumed evaluator.
