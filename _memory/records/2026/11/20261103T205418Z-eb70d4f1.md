Decision: `memory.sh prune` keeps any goal folder whose `progress.md` is a regular file with a `status:` other than `done`, reported as `keep:progress:<status>`, even when every archived record id is durable. Goal folders without `progress.md` keep the archive-only rule.

Details: the step runs after `keep:no-git`, `keep:symlink` and `keep:tracked` and before `keep:no-log` (first match wins). Status is the first whitespace-delimited token after the first `^status:` line, trailing CR stripped. `done` falls through to the archive rules. A token outside `[A-Za-z0-9._-]`, an empty value, or no `status:` line gives `keep:progress:unknown` (fail safe toward keeping). Research decisions are unchanged.

Why: since 2.3.0, feature Phase 2 runs Prune, then the unfinished-goal offer (which lists `progress.md` with status not `done`), then Recall. A goal that archived durable records in Phase 5 but was still in Phase 6 or 7 could be deleted by prune before the offer ever listed it. Reading the same field the offer reads keeps the two in agreement.

Alternatives considered: only override a would-delete verdict (keeps other keep reasons visible, but the reason then depends on archive state); infer "unfinished" from the orchestration log (second source of truth); keep unfinished goals only for N days after `updated:` (rejected for now; noted as follow-up).

Consequences: an abandoned goal that never reaches `status: done` is never pruned; the docs say it is kept until `status: done`.
