Pitfall: small wording additions to the canonical feature SKILL.template.md break suites that pin its size and line numbers, and the per-task suites do not show it.

What happened (goal resume-routing-followups): three stories each added required wording (a conditional phrase, a new subsection heading, a pointer). Every task's targeted suites passed, but the full run failed skill-slimming.sh, feature-feedback.sh and gate-hardening.sh, which cap the template at 11264 bytes (11346 after the changes; the base had 20 bytes of headroom). The new heading also moved the BOOTSTRAP[layer-skill-map] comment, breaking the line pin in skills/agent-kit/reference/bootstrap-ids.md checked by regression-proof-resume.sh (j).

How detected: rung 3 (run-all) for the budget; regression-proof-resume.sh (j) for the line pin.

Avoid it: before planning edits to SKILL.template.md, run `wc -c` against the 11264 budget and list bootstrap-ids.md rows pointing into the file; add skill-slimming.sh and regression-proof-resume.sh to every such task's targeted suites; trim with pointers to templates/ or reference/ rather than raising the budget (docs/agent-kit/guide/contributing.md); keep line breaks when trimming so pinned line numbers hold.
