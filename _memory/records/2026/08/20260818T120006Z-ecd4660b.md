## Decision

Amend CB1 semantics once: when rate-limit detection fires for an iteration, do not increment the `no_progress` counter; log a rate-limited pass and let the model breaker own recovery. All other CB1 and CB2 behavior stays unchanged.

## Why

Without the carve-out, three rate-limited iterations could trip CB1 and kill the run during the breaker cooldown, making the new breaker inert in practice.

## Rejected alternatives

- Strict no-touch policy on CB1-CB2 ( rejected by orchestrator scope amendment after goal judgment).
- Counting OPEN sleeps as no-progress (same failure mode).

## Consequences

Loop logs distinguish rate-limited passes from genuine lack of progress. Future concurrent dispatch designs must account for per-slot versus global CB1 semantics separately.
