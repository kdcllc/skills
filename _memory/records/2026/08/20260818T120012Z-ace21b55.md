## Decision

Remove the setup SKILL clause that gated checks-script PowerShell guidance on loop enabled or Windows as target platform. Guidance applies whenever the install adopts a structural checks script, consistent with mixed-OS teams and always emitting both loop drivers.

## Why

The gate contradicted the adjacent no OS-detection branching doctrine within the same SKILL section. Emitting a `.ps1` checks twin is cheap and harmless on Linux-only installs.

## Rejected alternatives

- Keep the OS gate and weaken the no-branching sentence (rejected—gate was the inconsistent half).
- Defer the decision to a docs-only edit (routed from windows-support Phase 6 to this polish story as a behavior-spec change).

## Consequences

Verify steps document skip-aware pwsh parser checks. Windows-support Phase 6 dropped the SKILL edit; story-006 owns the product behavior.
