## What happened

During STORY-012 close, `skills/setup/VERSION` legitimately stood at 0.13.0 while task-07 finished (manifest `kitVersion` matched; check J's `kitVersion==VERSION` join was green). Parallel editor sessions repeatedly reverted VERSION to 0.12.0, triggering red tripwires and coordination notes not to treat 0.13.0 as unauthorized.

## Root cause

Concurrent sessions reverted VERSION to 0.12.0 three times without knowing the active story's close write-set or the check-J join. Separately, another session's log misattributed the working-tree 0.13.0 to a root-owned process; it was STORY-012's authorized close write.

## How to avoid

Treat VERSION bumps authorized by the active story's close task as source of truth until the story lands. Before manual VERSION edits, run check J. The next story should take the semver after STORY-012's commit (0.14.0 planned for STORY-013), not roll back to satisfy stale working-tree assumptions.
