## Decision

Evaluator arbitration after unanimous same-item failure across cycles 1–3 uses a **new** model-map token for a frontier-tier, different-family arbiter. Do not reuse `{{MODEL_ALT_FAMILY}}`, which is reserved for light-tier implementer rotation during fix cycles.

## Why

Arbitration is a distinct cognitive job from implementer rotation; reusing the light-tier alt-family pin would under-power criteria review and blur tier policy.

## Rejected alternatives

- Reusing `{{MODEL_ALT_FAMILY}}` for the arbiter (explicitly forbidden in discovery).
- Parallel criteria mechanism separate from the signed contract (arbitration complements the v0.18 contract gate, not replaces it).

## Consequences

`models.map.md` and setup placeholder table must document the new token (check A). Dual-judge disagreement escalates to the human rather than auto-resolving. Criteria-defective arbitration outcomes escalate with the specific defect enumerated. v0.29.0 later allowed a flagged same-family arbiter when no cross-family frontier model is reachable.
