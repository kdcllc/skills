## Decision

When applying the minimum-evidence floor (fewer than five non-untestable cells blocks closure), count partial harness cells together with honored and untestable cells; disclose the partial versus honored composition in shipped docs.

## Why

Copilot probes cannot enforce file-read denial, so many cells are partial by rule, not by weak methodology. Treating partials as non-evidence would block closure after a successful user-authorized re-probe.

## Rejected alternatives

- Honored-only floor counting (would have kept the story blocked after nine informative cells).
- Upgrading partials to honored without confound justification.

## Consequences

Matrix and research annotations must state floor clearance with a composition caveat (for example, confound-capped partials). Readers should not interpret partial as full fidelity.
