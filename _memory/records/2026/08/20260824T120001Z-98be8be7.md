## Decision

When Phase 4 task execution exhausts three implement→evaluate fix cycles without PASS, the orchestrator continues through frozen rungs 4 (diagnosis-first re-approach), 5 (evaluator arbitration on the signed contract vs required-fixes history), and 6 (decomposition with selective retry) under hard guards, instead of unconditionally escalating. Human escalation remains terminal and immediate for bypass-class failures (destructive/irreversible work, security-sensitive surfaces, production/infrastructure changes).

## Why

Same-strategy fourth attempts have near-zero expected gain; strategy-changing rungs preserve human oversight capacity for cases that genuinely need it.

## Rejected alternatives

- Best-of-N parallel attempts (conflicts with loop disjoint write-set design; cost multiplier).
- Frontier + high-reasoning-effort pin rung (deferred — marginal for coding per research).
- Per-task token or cost budgets in markdown policy (not enforceable — must not be promised).

## Consequences

Feature skill and ORCHESTRATION template carry ladder vocabulary, guard limits, and escalation format including criteria-defective routing. Evaluator verdicts need failure-class tags. At most three additional implementation attempts after cycle 3; identical required-fixes lists halt the ladder. Loop driver attempt caps stay the outer backstop unchanged.
