## Decision

When the breaker is OPEN, the driver uses mechanism B: call `breaker_should_skip`, sleep the returned seconds with a pinned log line, call `breaker_should_skip` again to enter HALF-OPEN, then continue the same iteration—rather than skipping the whole iteration or restructuring the loop.

## Why

Early designs contradicted themselves on whether OPEN consumed iterations or could spin forever. Mechanism B keeps iteration accounting stable and matches the backlog’s skip-with-log requirement without a separate slept-pass concept.

## Rejected alternatives

- Skip entire iteration on OPEN (breaker never trials HALF-OPEN on schedule).
- Outer-loop sleep without second `should_skip` call (misses HALF-OPEN transition timing).

## Consequences

Log string `breaker OPEN — sleeping <N>s before HALF-OPEN trial` is byte-identical across bash and PowerShell twins. HALF-OPEN success counting (two successes to re-close) runs within normal iteration flow.
