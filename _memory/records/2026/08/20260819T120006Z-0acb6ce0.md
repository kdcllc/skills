## Question

How to prevent two agents writing the same file—at planning time rather than merge time—in an orchestrator/worker/evaluator kit?

## Load-bearing findings

- Field convergence: writes stay single-threaded per file; parallel agents contribute intelligence (review/read), not conflicting actions (Cognition Devin posts; Anthropic multi-agent research is read-heavy, ~90.2% gain at ~15× tokens for research breadth).
- AgenticFlict: ~27.7% conflict rate on agentic PRs—design away branch-and-merge coordination inside a run.
- CodeTeam SDS, ATM pre-write admission broker, and Co-Coder cohesion-aware partitioning provide machine-checkable ownership vocabulary.
- Commercial worktree/fleet models last-writer-wins on shared files—avoid as primary intra-run strategy.
- Recommendations: `writes:` / `reads:` on every task; static disjointness + acyclic deps before dispatch; allowed-paths fence; serialize hotspot files; interface-first scaffold-then-fill.

## Decisions taken

- Primary prevention in planning layer (write-sets) and prompt fences; tool path allowlists as defense-in-depth only.
- Kit direction aligned with STORY-008 ownership contracts (plan-time verification).

## Open questions

- Path-level tool enforcement still emerging across harnesses; ATM evidence is design-matrix not broad benchmark.
