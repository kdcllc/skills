## Decision

Move story selection, attempt increments, backlog transitions, and LEARNINGS/ESCALATIONS appends to the **driver**. Slots receive prompts with **`%%STORY_ID%%`** and **`%%OUTBOX_DIR%%`** substituted at dispatch (not setup-time `{{TOKEN}}`s). Admission allows concurrent stories only when `writes:` globs are disjoint and neither holds a declared hotspot path; protected-path overlap refuses dispatch for the run.

## Why

Concurrent iterations otherwise compute the same eligible story and corrupt shared `_goals/` files. Check B scans emitted `_loop/` for `{{…}}`; `%%…%%` delimiters avoid false positives while remaining visible in templates.

## Rejected alternatives

- On-disk claim files or lock directories (design rejects; single-threaded driver control flow suffices).
- In-worktree outboxes only (breaks flag=0 uniformity; mktemp outboxes match existing output-file pattern).

## Consequences

Manual `/auto-loop` without driver pins keeps legacy self-select behavior. N>1 requires worktree isolation (`LOOP_WORKTREE=1`). Integrator runs pre-merge rebase so sibling branches can ff-merge under disjoint writes.
