## Decision

Adopt a three-layer hybrid for bounded-concurrency auto-loop: (1) planning-layer write-set admission and disjointness judging, (2) driver-owned slot dispatch and supervision, (3) integration mechanics (worktree or equivalent) with explicit fold-back rules for shared files such as backlog and LEARNINGS.

## Why

Slot arithmetic alone does not address inter-story write collisions, breaker races, or completion aggregation. The research doc argues against locks and post-hoc branch merge as primary strategies; the design must reconcile that counter-position with real concurrent loop behavior.

## Rejected alternatives

- Worktree-per-story with merge repair as the primary conflict strategy (treated as high conflict rate, contradicts planning-time prevention thesis).
- Pure serialized writes with parallel read-only judging only (recorded as a seriously considered alternative in Rejected alternatives).
- Implementing dispatch in the same story (explicitly out of scope; sized as roughly three to four follow-up stories).

## Consequences

STORY-008 implements plan-layer contracts; STORY-009+ harden breaker IO; STORY-010–012 implement slots, driver dispatch, and exit aggregation per later backlog items. Design status remains proposal until each section is marked shipped by its story.
