## Decision

When breaker state is present but still invalid after a CR-tolerant terminator check and up to three short re-read retries, `breaker_load` seeds **OPEN** with `openedAt=now` and a full cooldown—preserving parsed `chain`, `fallbackIndex`, and `currentModel` (derived as `chain[fallbackIndex]` when absent)—instead of reseeding fresh CLOSED.

## Why

A torn read during atomic write could erase an active OPEN state and return the loop to the most expensive model mid-rate-limit. Failing safe costs one cooldown; failing open erased protection silently.

## Rejected alternatives

- Keep reseed-to-CLOSED on any parse failure (status quo; judged unsafe).
- Blindly set `currentModel` to `chain[0]` on fail-safe (would re-pin the rate-limited model).

## Consequences

Hand-corrupted state files incur a full cooldown until deleted; harness assertions 11/11b were updated to match. Atomic write uses sibling temp files then rename in both twins.
