Fence-delivery checks often rely on an orchestrator-authored line in the goal's orchestration log claiming that prompts or write-set instructions were dispatched. Until STORY-012 addendum 1 landed durable dispatch-prompt capture (shipped v0.13.0), that log line was the primary evidence — and it could be satisfied without a matching diff (claim-without-substance).

Evaluators should sanity-check claim versus actual file changes, not merely presence of the log sentence.
