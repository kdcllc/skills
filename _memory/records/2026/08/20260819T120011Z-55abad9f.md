Several stories concurrently touch the same canonical templates — notably `skills/setup/templates/ORCHESTRATION.md` and `skills/setup/templates/skills/feature/SKILL.md` (claimed across STORY-008, STORY-011, and STORY-012 work). Define contracts around these hotspots up front and never co-dispatch tasks whose `writes:` intersect on them.
