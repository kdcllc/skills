## What changed

- **Dossier:**
  - `LOOP_DOSSIER` (default on, `off` disables record, inject and clear) writes `$PWD/_memory/loop/attempts/<story-id>.md` (gitignored; emit adds the line);
  - it keeps the 2 newest entries (each ≤ 2048 bytes, with a frozen format: exit status, tamper, failure signature `<outcome>-<cksum>`, diffstat captured right after `_loop_reap_diff_stats`, and slot notes from `<outbox>/attempt.md` capped at 1500 bytes);
  - it is fence-safe at capture and at inject, injected as a fenced DATA block on the next dispatch, and deleted when a reap sets `passes: true`.
- **Breaker:**
  - four bash-only keys (`probeAfter`, `probeBackoff`, `probing`, `preProbeIndex`), and `_breaker_write` arguments 11–14 are optional, so 10-argument callers keep working;
  - `breaker_probe` runs 1800 s after a fallback; success stays on chain[0]; failure restores the pre-probe index; backoff is `min(max(b,1800)*2,14400)`;
  - `breaker.ps1` is frozen and tolerates and drops the extra keys.
- **`claude-stream`:** opt-in, splices `--output-format stream-json --verbose`, detects rate limits from typed `system`/`api_retry` JSON lines (`^\{`-anchored, three herestring stages, no inter-grep pipes), and falls back to text over stderr plus the final result envelope only; `auto` never picks it.
- **Tamper:** one awk pass per scanned file (EREs through `ENVIRON`), and the default classifier excludes `fixtures/` segments and `*.golden` / `*.snap`.
- **Guards and tests:**
  - `checks.sh` check E and `escalation-ladder.sh` check 5 exempt the exact `claude-stream` token;
  - new suite `tests/agent-kit/templates/loop-retry-breaker.sh` (18 cases, mutation-proven);
  - 100 goldens regenerated.
