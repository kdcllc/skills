## Goal

fix-cycle-provenance: make it visible and routable when a task evaluator changes its asks across fix cycles, so moving targets stop being contained only by the 3-cycle cap. Branch feat/loop-fix, on top of loop-guards (33d40b3).

## Outcome

Shipped uncommitted under agent-kit `## [Unreleased]` (no version bump, no PR, per the user).

- **Provenance tags.** On every re-evaluation verdict (any task verdict after the first, ladder re-evaluations, and every final-audit re-audit after the first), each required fix carries the kind tag and then one provenance tag: `[carried]` (still unmet; wording may sharpen, the ask may not grow), `[regression]` (caused by the fix; cite file:line), or `[new]`. Rules live in task-criteria § Provenance tags; evaluator.md has only the format line and a pointer (its Tuning note keeps rules in the criteria skills).
- **Justification clause.** A `[new]` item carries ` — missed earlier: <reason>` directly before the kind tag. A reversal of an earlier required fix carries ` — reverses cycle N: <verbatim earlier item>` instead, and the verdict is `failure_class: criteria-defect` naming both items. Phase 5 has no ladder, so a re-audit reversal escalates at detection.
- **Orchestrator side** (fix-cycles § Provenance). Re-evaluation packages carry every earlier required-fixes list verbatim, so tagging also works on stateless or fresh spawns. A tag check (skipped for destructive/security/infra verdicts) does one round trip that consumes no cycle. Every `[new]` item gets a `late finding:` log note, and late findings earn no extra cycle.
- **Ladder.** Rung 5 also runs on a drift trigger: ≥ 2 `[new]` items across the cycle 2–3 verdicts. The unanimity and repeated-signature comparisons strip kind tags, provenance tags, and the justification clause (from its start through the end of the item). The diagnostician reads tags as evidence. With `ladder: escalate`, option 1 stays "Run the continuation ladder" and the Ladder line adds "rung 5 runs first".
- **Packaging.** The feature-skill Resume policy and the ORCHESTRATION re-check exception were reworded so they no longer say deltas list "only applied fixes"; Phase 3 is excluded. A new guard, `tests/agent-kit/templates/fix-cycle-provenance.sh`, has 7 canonical checks, 8 mutation proofs, and a fresh-emit check. The evaluator Arbitration-mode Trigger also names the drift trigger. Goldens were regenerated. The full suite passes 38/38.
