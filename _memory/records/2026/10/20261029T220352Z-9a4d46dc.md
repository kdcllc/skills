When a goal introduces a new required artifact or format and adds a transition rule for goals planned before the upgrade, put the carve-out (or a pointer to it) in every phase that checks the new artifact, not only the phase where the rule lives.

In consumer-trace the transition rule lived in pre-flight check 2, with a goal-criteria pointer. But the Phase 5 consumer check in ORCHESTRATION and the evaluator had no carve-out. A legacy goal would have hit a blocking F6 finding with no valid fix: the prescribed amendment fails lint on a goal with no `## Consumers` section. The final audit caught it. Grep every template that names the new artifact and confirm each one either handles legacy goals or points to the rule.
