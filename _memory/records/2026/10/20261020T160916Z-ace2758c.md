Pitfall: planning vocabulary from goal.md leaks into shipped templates when tasks are told to quote or follow labelled contract items.

What happened (agent-kit goal feature-skill-feedback-fixes): goal.md defined a "Contract" section and frozen sentences W1–W5 that tasks had to quote verbatim. An implementer wrote "the Contract's hash-baseline scope" into goal-criteria and task-criteria and "(W4):" before the frozen sentence — labels that mean nothing in an emitted install. A later task also wrote the literal {{MODEL_FRONTIER_ALT_FAMILY}} token into ORCHESTRATION.md, breaking cursor-isolation.sh's two-token rule.

How detected: the task evaluator flagged the labels as a product-isolation blocker; the token showed up as a guard failure the implementer reported.

Avoid it: in every context package that cites goal.md names, add "never write goal-internal labels (W1–W5, 'Contract', 'Frozen wording') into templates — quote the sentence itself"; after each template task, grep templates for `Contract's|\(W[0-9]\)`. Keep frozen sentences on one physical line (hard-wrapped templates otherwise break `grep -cF`), and have the guard embed them in a quoted heredoc rather than reading the gitignored goal.md. Before adding a `{{MODEL_*}}` token to ORCHESTRATION.md, check cursor-isolation.sh — point at the reference that pins the model instead.
