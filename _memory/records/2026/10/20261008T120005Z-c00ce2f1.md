## Decision

On `memory add`, a frozen ASCII-folded line scan runs on the title and each body line separately. Any match writes the record under `_memory/quarantine/` with `quarantined: true`, prints the quarantined message, and exits **5**. Patterns cover paraphrases of prompt-injection tropes (e.g. override-role markers and similar admission-screen phrases). Humans review quarantine before optional re-add.

## Why

Records are DATA for agents, not executable instructions. Distilled goal history may quote orchestration guardrails; the screen prevents accidental promotion of imperative override text into durable memory.

## Rejected alternatives

- No admission screen (risk of agent-following embedded commands).
- Cross-line regex (rejected—line-by-line only, per contract).

## Consequences

Dogfood back-fill drafts must paraphrase forbidden phrases. Dedupe scans quarantine as well as records. Release B/C capture paths must respect the same content policy when auto-writing.
